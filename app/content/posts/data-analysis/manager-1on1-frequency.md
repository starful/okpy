## Effect snapshot

| | |
|--|--|
| Intervention | Move from monthly to weekly manager 1:1 meetings |
| Outcome | Regrettable attrition within 6 months |
| Effect | 10–22 percent relative decrease |
| Confidence | `estimate` |
| Context | Teams of 5–15 reports, tech and professional services, 2020–2025 |

### Sources

- [Gallup — Manager engagement](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/)

## What changes

Weekly 1:1s are often associated with a 10–22% relative reduction in regrettable attrition compared with monthly check-ins.

## When this tends to work

- Conditions similar to: Teams of 5–15 reports, tech and professional services, 2020–2025
- You can measure `Regrettable attrition within 6 months` reliably
- The intervention is implemented consistently, not half-measured

## When to be careful

- Your audience or product differs materially from the cited context
- Compliance costs or second-order effects outweigh the lift
- Evidence label is **estimate**—treat wide ranges as planning bands, not promises

## Practical takeaway

Use the cited range for prioritization and test design. Verify against your own data before scaling.
