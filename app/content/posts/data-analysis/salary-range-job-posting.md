## Effect snapshot

| | |
|--|--|
| Intervention | Publish salary band on public job postings |
| Outcome | Qualified applicants per opening |
| Effect | 15–30 percent relative increase |
| Confidence | `study` |
| Context | US job boards post-pay-transparency laws, 2023–2025 |

### Sources

- [LinkedIn — Salary transparency](https://www.linkedin.com/business/talent/blog)

## What changes

Visible salary bands are commonly associated with a 15–30% relative increase in qualified applicants per role.

## When this tends to work

- Conditions similar to: US job boards post-pay-transparency laws, 2023–2025
- You can measure `Qualified applicants per opening` reliably
- The intervention is implemented consistently, not half-measured

## When to be careful

- Your audience or product differs materially from the cited context
- Compliance costs or second-order effects outweigh the lift
- Evidence label is **study**—treat wide ranges as planning bands, not promises

## Practical takeaway

Use the cited range for prioritization and test design. Verify against your own data before scaling.
