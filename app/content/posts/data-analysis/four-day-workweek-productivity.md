## Effect snapshot

| | |
|--|--|
| Intervention | Pilot 32-hour four-day week with same pay for 6+ months |
| Outcome | Revenue or output per employee |
| Effect | 0–8 percent relative increase |
| Confidence | `study` |
| Context | UK and Iceland pilots, knowledge work, 2022–2025 |

### Sources

- [4 Day Week Global — Trial results](https://4dayweek.com/)

## What changes

Well-run four-day pilots often report flat to +8% relative output with higher wellbeing—results vary by industry.

## When this tends to work

- Conditions similar to: UK and Iceland pilots, knowledge work, 2022–2025
- You can measure `Revenue or output per employee` reliably
- The intervention is implemented consistently, not half-measured

## When to be careful

- Your audience or product differs materially from the cited context
- Compliance costs or second-order effects outweigh the lift
- Evidence label is **study**—treat wide ranges as planning bands, not promises

## Practical takeaway

Use the cited range for prioritization and test design. Verify against your own data before scaling.
