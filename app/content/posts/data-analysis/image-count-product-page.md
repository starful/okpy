## Effect snapshot

| | |
|--|--|
| Intervention | Increase product gallery from 3–4 images to 8+ (multiple angles, in-use shots) |
| Outcome | Add-to-cart rate |
| Effect | 6–18 percent relative increase |
| Confidence | `study` |
| Context | E-commerce PDPs, fashion and consumer goods, 2022–2025 |

### Sources

- [Baymard — Product page images](https://baymard.com/blog/product-image-categories)

## What changes

Richer product image galleries are commonly associated with a 6–18% relative lift in add-to-cart on PDPs.

## When this tends to work

- Conditions similar to: E-commerce PDPs, fashion and consumer goods, 2022–2025
- You can measure `Add-to-cart rate` reliably
- The intervention is implemented consistently, not half-measured

## When to be careful

- Your audience or product differs materially from the cited context
- Compliance costs or second-order effects outweigh the lift
- Evidence label is **study**—treat wide ranges as planning bands, not promises

## Practical takeaway

Use the cited range for prioritization and test design. Verify against your own data before scaling.
