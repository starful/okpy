## Effect snapshot

| | |
|--|--|
| Intervention | Practice 10–20 minutes guided meditation daily for 8 weeks |
| Outcome | Perceived stress scale scores |
| Effect | 10–25 percent relative decrease |
| Confidence | `meta_analysis` |
| Context | Healthy adults, app-guided programs, 2015–2024 RCTs |

### Sources

- [APA — Meditation research](https://www.apa.org/topics/mindfulness/meditation)

## What changes

Short daily meditation programs are often associated with a 10–25% relative drop in perceived stress scores over 8 weeks.

## When this tends to work

- Conditions similar to: Healthy adults, app-guided programs, 2015–2024 RCTs
- You can measure `Perceived stress scale scores` reliably
- The intervention is implemented consistently, not half-measured

## When to be careful

- Your audience or product differs materially from the cited context
- Compliance costs or second-order effects outweigh the lift
- Evidence label is **meta_analysis**—treat wide ranges as planning bands, not promises

## Practical takeaway

Use the cited range for prioritization and test design. Verify against your own data before scaling.
