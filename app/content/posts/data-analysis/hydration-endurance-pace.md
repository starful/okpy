## Effect snapshot

| | |
|--|--|
| Intervention | Follow structured fluid intake vs drinking ad hoc during 10K–half marathon |
| Outcome | Pace decline in final 25% of race |
| Effect | 5–12 percent relative decrease |
| Confidence | `study` |
| Context | Amateur endurance runners, mild climates, 2018–2024 |

### Sources

- [ACSM — Hydration guidelines](https://www.acsm.org/)

## What changes

Structured hydration is commonly linked to 5–12% less late-race pace decay in amateur endurance events.

## When this tends to work

- Conditions similar to: Amateur endurance runners, mild climates, 2018–2024
- You can measure `Pace decline in final 25% of race` reliably
- The intervention is implemented consistently, not half-measured

## When to be careful

- Your audience or product differs materially from the cited context
- Compliance costs or second-order effects outweigh the lift
- Evidence label is **study**—treat wide ranges as planning bands, not promises

## Practical takeaway

Use the cited range for prioritization and test design. Verify against your own data before scaling.
