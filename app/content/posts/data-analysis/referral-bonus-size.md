| Confidence | `estimate` |

### Sources

- [ReferralCandy — Referral benchmarks](https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/referral-marketing-statistics)

## What changes

Larger dual-sided referral incentives are commonly linked to a 15–40% relative increase in referral participation.

## When this tends to work

- Conditions similar to: Consumer apps and fintech, US market, 2021–2025
- You can measure `Referral program participation rate` reliably
- The intervention is implemented consistently, not half-measured

## When to be careful

- Your audience or product differs materially from the cited context
- Compliance costs or second-order effects outweigh the lift
- Evidence label is **estimate**—treat wide ranges as planning bands, not promises

## Practical takeaway

Use the cited range for prioritization and test design. Verify against your own data before scaling.
